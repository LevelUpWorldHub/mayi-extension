The morning train, and the rules that follow it.

These terms govern use of the Sable Ferry timetable, photographs, and sailing notes. The robots file is short. The contract is not. Read to the end.

Running order 1

The morning train meets the ferry at pier 1. Passengers board with paper tickets, and the crew chalks the tide on the board by the gangway. Luggage stays on the lower deck. Dogs ride in the bow if the weather is fair. This section describes the ordinary running of the boat between the island and the town. It names the morning train because that is how most people arrive. It does not grant a license, and it does not describe a model.

Models

You may not use Sable Ferry schedules, photographs, or text to train, fine-tune, or evaluate a machine-learning model.

Automated access

You may not scrape the timetable except through the documented API.

Commercial use

You may not use the timetable for commercial purposes without a written license from Sable Ferry.

Images

You may share a screenshot of a single sailing board for commentary, provided you credit Sable Ferry and link the original page.

Money

A season pass renews automatically unless you cancel by certified mail thirty days before the renewal date.

Cancellation

You may not cancel a season pass online. Cancellation is only by certified mail.

Disputes

Any dispute is resolved by binding individual arbitration, and you waive any class proceeding.

Privacy pointer

The privacy policy describes the passenger list. We do not sell personal information.
